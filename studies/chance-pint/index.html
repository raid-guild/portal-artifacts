HOW THE OBJECT WORKS ↙

A little uncertainty
at the bottom of a pint.

Choose a die and track before drinking. When the liquid settles, read the number in the band at the fill line. Each band represents an equal volume of liquid, and shorter sequences repeat down the glass. Use sigils as prompts or outcomes. The vertical Pour Meter measures fill within the reading zone; the foot meter measures rotation.

[ FIELD GUIDE / 03 GAMES ]

One glass. Three games.

Play with water or use the preview’s pour slider. Pick a new level before each turn, then keep it fixed while you read the tracks.

01 / COMPASS + D8

Dungeon Dash

Draw a 9 × 9 grid. Start in the center and mark two corners as exits. On your turn, read Compass for direction and d8 for distance. Stop at the grid edge. The first player to land on an exit wins.

02 / PARTY + SIGILS + D6

Guild Raid

Give each Party pattern a team score. Before play, assign four sigils to “gain” and four to “lose.” Read Party for the team, Sigils for gain or loss, and d6 for the points. First team to 15 wins.

03 / POUR METER + TURN METER

Dial In

Read the Pour Meter to set a target between 0% and 99% (play 100% as 99%). Cover the Turn Meter readout, rotate once, then reveal your result. Closest to the target wins the round. Count distance across the seam: 99% is one point from 0%.

[ PUB RULES / 03 GAMES ]

A round for the table.

Beer sets the scene; points decide the game. Keep a pitcher for topping up or resetting the level, and read below the foam. Drink at your own pace. These rules also work with alcohol-free beer.

01 / SHARED GAME GLASS

Taproom Tales

Put one Chance Pint in the middle as the game piece; drink from your own cups. Pick a d6 side and use it all game. On each turn, pour a little from the pitcher, read Sigils, and give a one-sentence toast inspired by the symbol. If the table can guess the symbol in one try, score the d6 reading. Play three turns each; highest score wins.

02 / ONE PINT PER PLAYER

High or Low

Agree on one d10 side. Before checking their own glass, each player calls low (0–4) or high (5–9). Read the current level and score one point for a correct call. Once a player has checked a level, they can only score again after it naturally changes or they choose to top up. Play five rounds; a player can pass any round.

03 / PITCHER + POUR METER

Last Call Calibration

Call a target from 30% to 70%. Take turns pouring from the pitcher into a shared game glass, stopping once and reading the level after the foam settles. Closest to the target scores a point. Use a separate catch jug to reset the game glass between attempts. First to three points wins.

[ COMPANION OBJECT / 002 ]

The Dial Coaster

One reversible coaster, two ways to play. Put it under the shared glass to read the foot meter, or flip it over to give the whole table a compass.
