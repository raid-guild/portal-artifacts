## Current rule

Blue water tiles and arrows show each path's direction. Symbols in the water swap and match normally. An authored level may have one to four disjoint routes, including neighboring lanes and nested rings. After an accepted turn's normal clear, fall, and refill, every occupant on every active route moves one cell simultaneously from the same board snapshot. A route may close as an ordinary loop or jump between paired outer edges; the edge jump is shown as a wrap, not as a line crossing the board. The moved board resolves matches before checking crate exits; any resulting clears, falls, and deliveries animate. A carried power does not fire merely because it moved; a power newly created this turn remains protected. Each active route moves only once per accepted turn, including direct power activation. Invalid swaps, hints, reshuffles, and the coin finale do not advance routes.

Any salt crust remaining on a route freezes that route only. A second, unblocked route keeps moving; off-route crust stops neither. The last block may break in the normal clear of a turn; its route then moves in that same turn. Salt broken in the post-current cascade releases the route next turn. A frosted route stays visibly stopped until the salt-break animation completes. Upper loops can carry crates upward, but cargo still exits only when it falls to a valid bottom edge of the board. Every cargo mask retains bottom exits with no hidden internal exit directly under a crate column.
