# Chapter 8: Water Collection Station

This is the first playable version of the second oasis chapter. It follows the River Journey and keeps the existing five matching symbols. The route returns west to the water collection station, then points toward the twin towers and balloon landing zone. The chapter remains open to playtest tuning.

## Story and companion

Skiff brings the party upstream to the second oasis. The Artificer's brass drones tend the collection nets, but dry mesh cannot fill the balloon reservoirs. Restoring the marked collector ground opens the next sky route. The Artificer joins the Raid Party on arrival; the current active member remains active until the player selects someone else. As an active member, the Artificer charges one Prism from eleven keys.

The character's short notes draw on the RaidGuild Field Guide's Artificer theme of bounded automation and checking outputs. In-game dialogue is original story text, not a quotation from the guide.

## Condensation rule

Marked net squares are fixed terrain beneath the falling symbols. A few begin wet. A match that includes an already wet square coats all matched net squares. A power swapped off wet mesh carries water into its effect, even if it lands on a dry square. A dry Jet picks up water where each ray crosses a wet net, coating marked squares from that point onward along that ray. The opposite ray and earlier squares stay dry unless they encounter their own wet net. A combo containing any wet power coats its entire effect, including the beams or areas of every power copied by a Prism merge. Salt on a net blocks moisture until every layer breaks. A wet power can crack the salt but cannot wet the net beneath it in that same clear wave; a later wet hit can coat the exposed net. An ordinary wet match can coat a net on the same wave that it breaks the last salt layer. Combos and chains read the wet terrain from before the clear wave, while later cascades see newly wet squares. A Tile Bomb alone does not spread water. Wet net progress is permanent within an attempt, and all nets must be wet to finish.

The starting tutorial demonstrates a wet match and a wet-origin power. The board uses a filled wet meter rather than treating collectors as ordinary symbol pickups. The standalone playtest prototype remains memory-only and does not write campaign progress.

## Balance and access notes

All ten levels need no-booster winning routes for every selectable companion after each authored balance change. These are reachability checks, not human difficulty measurements. Check the new salt-on-net stages in the next human playtest before changing move budgets. Chapter 8 uses the Dune Pass access rule. Its saves, rewards, and attempts use the normal campaign systems; the playable prototype remains separate.
