# Condensation mechanic experiment

This is one memory-only playtest board at `/dune-relay/condensation/?playtest=1`. It is linked from playtest Settings. The production build shows an unavailable page and never mounts the board. The experiment has no campaign level ID, save, wallet, reward, or cloud request.

A 5×5 collector patch sits in the center of a 9×9 board. Three collectors start wet; the goal is to wet the remaining 22 in 26 moves. Wetness belongs to collector squares, so falling, swapping, and refilling tiles do not move it. A connected match with any wet square wets every collector in that connected match, including a square retaining a newly created power. A power fired from a wet square wets collector squares in its hit footprint. A dry power crossing a wet square remains dry. Each clear wave uses the wet terrain snapshot from before that wave, so a power newly touched by wetness cannot spread until a later wave. Tile Bomb alone does not spread water. The board has no currents, salt, crates, purchases, or on-demand tools.

The level reuses River art, ordinary tiles, Keeper power, and sound. Dotted collector borders turn into a pale silver-blue film with bubble clusters, wavelets, and short droplets. The HUD shows covered collectors as `3 / 25 WET` at the start, with a filling tank. Retry rebuilds the same seeded board in memory. The nine-move witness in `scripts/fixtures/condensation-winning-route.json` proves that the board can be won without boosters; it does not establish difficulty. The targeted core checks in `scripts/check-condensation.cjs` cover match connectivity, wet and dry power effects, pre-wave chaining, no-spread Tile Bomb behavior, final-move victory, and the witness.
