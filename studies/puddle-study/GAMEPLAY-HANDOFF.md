Level 1: Gathering Garden. Quiet tutorial: descend two short steps from the raised start → move left on the upper lane → collect contact gold → touch green flesh to grow → surround a stationary gem (holding Space builds a mound) → descend the left stairs → return right on the lower lane through two transverse columns of ten narrow-gap pillars → release to flow under a low lintel → collect more gems → enter an exit funnel. ## Weight Garden and storey transition

Level 2 is a three-height switchback in local simulation coordinates, rendered one storey lower at world Y -7.2. The high rear lane is Y 2.16 at Z ≤ -1.3; the middle terrace is Y 1.08 for -1.3 < Z ≤ 2; the low front lane is Y 0 at Z > 2. The left stair corridor spans X -8.8 to -6.2 and descends four 0.27 ramps toward positive Z from -1.3 to 0.1. The right corridor spans X 6.2 to 8.8 and descends four more from Z 2 to 3.4. Exposed ledges remain optional shortcuts, but their low sides block upward teleportation. Rendered floor bands and stair pieces sample the same terrain descriptor as collision.

The Level 2 start (7.6, -2.8) is on its highest terrace and need not align with Level 1's drain. On Continue, a staggered render-only pink stream pours vertically through a small decorative chute mouth above the new high start; an unseen route connects it to the previous drain, while green supply stays grounded. Restarting Level 2 pours directly above its own start. The stream begins 2.3 local units above landing, then settles with neutral physics after 1.1 seconds of arrival and 0.42 seconds of input lock. The camera descends 7.2 world units without reversing the board or input axes.

The middle basin is at (-2.5, 0.35), radius 1.15 and depth 0.75 below Y 1.08. The weight gate is centered at X 1.5, with a Z -0.8 to 1.5 passage at middle height. Hold F at the basin rim to shed real body particles. At least 48 settled shed particles on its bottom plate lift the gate by 0.58; it releases below 36 and waits while the elevated passage is occupied. The route moves left across the high rear lane, down the left stairs, around the basin and through the gate to the right, down the right stairs, then left across the low front lane to the exit at (-7.6, 3.65). Four pools provide 232 loose particles; three gems and 17 gold pieces trace the full path. Both gardens have independent pickup state and result totals, and Level 1's completed score remains in memory on Continue.

`GARDEN` remains the Level 1 alias for older tests. `WEIGHT_GARDEN`, `PASSAGE_GARDEN`, `REACH_GARDEN`, `GRIP_GARDEN`, and `GARDEN_LEVELS` define five independent puzzles. Physics uses local Y coordinates; views, the pink body, green supply, and debug particles apply `-7.2 × (level ID - 1)` only while rendering. The multi-funnel visibility check uses the combined terrain height so shallow channels and basins do not split the protected coating. Art assets and sky remain shared. Each lower facade is placed without mirroring or a Z offset; each translated exit collar follows its own authored exit.

## Grip Garden

Level 5 keeps the three terraces, with the first drop moved to Z -2.1 and the left stair run shifted 0.8 toward the high start. It begins at (7.6, -3.2) at Y 2.16 and exits at (-7.6, 3.65) at Y 0. Four 58-particle pools are distributed along the route. On the middle terrace, a sage ramp at X -1.65 to -0.55 and Z -1.35 to 1.25 rises eastward from Y 1.08 to 1.44. The solid platform spans X -0.55 to 1.65, Z -1.95 to 1.85, with a top at Y 3.78. Both its west and east faces have full-height ribbed grip. Press D from the west ramp or A from the east to climb: the real core must touch the exterior face with its living coat and nearby connected non-coat support, while attached particles follow over the lip. Greater connected mass climbs more slowly. Releasing lets the body sag. The top supports brain and contraction targets only after they physically cross above its collision plane; a body beneath it cannot be lifted through. The elevated middle gem at (0.55, -0.05) still requires real flesh coverage, even with a full 297-particle body. A smooth aqua strip at X 3 to 5.4, Z -2.1 to 2 reduces braking and floor friction so momentum can carry the body over the middle ledge. Elsewhere the original floor response remains. This level has no shedding or tendrils. The view and body render at world Y -28.8, with a fifth façade/chute/collar and only Level 4 briefly visible during descent. Material studies offers **Grip & Slide** to practice the actual level directly without changing the campaign's Level 1 start. The final campaign card reports five independent scores.

**Campaign design rule:** each playable storey starts at its highest terrace, then turns through visible depth with meaningful descent. A chute or transition can bridge different X/Z positions between storeys; future levels should not flatten into a single same-height board.
