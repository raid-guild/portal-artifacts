# Social cards

The published `field-guide-social-v3.png` is a 1200 × 630 composition generated
by `make-social-v3.cjs`. It uses the approved `field-guide-cover-moloch-v1.png`,
the checked-in crossed-swords logo, and local Louchi fonts and colors. It
matches the current website's hero art and headline. Rebuild it with
`NODE_PATH=./print/node_modules node art/make-social-v3.cjs` from the study
directory after installing the pinned Playwright tool in `print/`, then run
`node build.mjs`. It is a designed share card, not a captured
gameplay or policy screenshot.

The earlier `field-guide-social-v1.png` was composed by `make-social.py` from
first-party RaidGuild website landscape art. It is retained as a local concept,
not the current published preview.

The v3 card uses native Last Duel Bold Extended 2 and Bold Retalic cuts supplied by Louchi. The prior v2 card remains as a historical output.
