# Forest monster art

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-03. These are original game interpretations of the curated MonsterMaps traits, not original NFT artwork. The canonical on-chain sheets remain separate in the Monster Book.

Production files in `public/sprites/monsters/` are 256 × 256 RGBA PNGs, resized with nearest-neighbor sampling and lossless PNG compression. Transparency is preserved. Render with nearest-neighbor filtering, and mirror horizontally for left-facing movement. Full-resolution originals were retained in the task workspace's `outputs/raid-survivor-forest-art/` directory.
