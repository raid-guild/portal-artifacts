# Art attribution

Ranger, Wizard, Dwarf, Rogue, Necromancer, Warrior, and Alchemist pixel sprite sheets, character preview images, and the item sheet (including ham) were copied from the [Raid Guild Roundtable Melee repository](https://github.com/raid-guild/arcade-roundtable-melee), reference commit `cd503cbbf56e5db4fcc592ed11fe5d9cf710afcc`, under `public/sprites/characters/` and `public/sprites/items.png`. The game uses horizontal left/right poses from the ten-frame character sheets. Enemy factions tint and scale the Rogue, Necromancer, Warrior, and Alchemist art.

Moloch is original generated artwork. The generation prompt and asset provenance are saved in [art/moloch-generation.txt](./art/moloch-generation.txt).

The arena floor, runes, effects, menus, and HUD are original code rendered with Canvas 2D, Three.js, and CSS.

Cinzel and DM Sans are self-hosted through Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License. System fallbacks are included.

The Raid Survivor social card and Portal cover are original generated artwork.
Its generation brief is saved in [art/social-card-generation.txt](./art/social-card-generation.txt).
The card is promotional key art rather than a gameplay screenshot.
